# Changelog

All notable changes to **Tadah.LAMMPS** (the `pair_tadah` LAMMPS pair-style integration) are documented here.
Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

## [1.6.0] — unreleased

### Added
- `pair_coeff` ignores the training record that `tadah train`, `tadah hpo` and `tadah refit` write into a potential (a block of comment lines, opened by `# --- training record (no key is read from it) ---`, ahead of the key lines): a potential with the record loads to the same context as one without it, and none of the keys the record lists, `VERBOSE` among them, is read. Test: `test_training_record_load.cpp`.
- Tests: `pair_coeff` (`LammpsTadah`) loads a D2_EAM + DM_EAM potential that `core::Context::write_potential` wrote with 6 and with 3 significant digits (`NUMERIC 6`, `NUMERIC 3`): Tadah.CORE writes the cutoffs and radial grids exactly whatever NUMERIC is, so the potential records the setfl file's cutoff, which D2_EAM and DM_EAM compare exactly. `test_numeric_exact_keys_load.cpp`.
- Tests: **`pair_style tadah` on several MPI ranks**, `tadah.lammps_mpi_<case>` (`ctest -L mpi`, `ctest -L lammps`; `tests/mpi/check_pair_tadah_mpi.py`). Each entry runs an `lmp` built with `-DBUILD_MPI=ON` and ML-TADAH (`TADAH_LMP_MPI`) under the launcher of its MPI (`TADAH_LMP_MPIEXEC`; flags before `-n`: `TADAH_LMP_MPIEXEC_PREFLAGS`) on 1, 2 and 4 ranks of a 1024-atom cell and on 4 ranks in a `4 1 1` grid of a 250-atom cell, whose sub-domains are narrower than the ghost cutoff, and compares each run by atom id with a reference run (the serial `lmp` of `TADAH_LMP_SERIAL` when it is set, else the MPI `lmp` on one rank): at step 0 the forces (to 1e-8 eV/Å), per-atom energies and virials, the energy and the pressure tensor, after 100 NVE steps the positions, velocities and energy, to round-off (the script states each tolerance and why); each run must report its rank count and grid, rebuild its neighbour lists at least once and run the kernel the case names. Cases: `quickstart` (a copy of the Quick Start potential, D2_Blip + DM_mBlipEnv: the generic many-body kernel `compute_2b_mb_half`, newton on and off), `two_species` (two LAMMPS types and elements in that kernel), `dm_ream` (D2_Knot5 + DM_REAM: the EAM fast path and, with `EAMFAST false`, the generic kernel, newton on and off), `nonlinear` (M_BLR BF_Polynomial2: the full-list kernel `compute_2b_mb_full`), `pair_2b` (`compute_2b_fast` and `compute_2b`). Without `TADAH_LMP_MPI` or `TADAH_LMP_MPIEXEC` the entries are Skipped (exit code 77), naming what is missing; a variable that names no executable is a failure. New fixtures: `tests_data/pots/ta_quickstart_blip_env.tadah`, `tanb_blip_env_any_pair.tadah`, `ta_poly2_blip_env.tadah`. These cases needed no change to `pair_tadah`: every kernel above agrees with the serial run on 1, 2 and 4 ranks, newton on and off (the full-list kernel newton on only, which it requires). Checked by hand, the generic, EAM fast-path and full-list kernels also agree on 8 ranks (`2 2 2` and `8 1 1` grids) and, on 1, 2 and 4 ranks, with `comm_style tiled`, on a triclinic cell and at 6000 K (where the trajectories drift apart as two serial runs in different atom order do), and the generic kernel with D2_EAM + DM_EAM on 1, 2 and 4 ranks; the pair kernels were run on 1, 2 and 4 ranks only. No ghost-communication defect was found. A `pair_tadah` without the generic kernel's reverse comm, without the fast path's forward comm, or that packs the wrong dF/drho component for the forward comm fails the cases of that kernel. The kernel of a `DIMER` potential is covered at step 0 by the `dimer` case (next entry); its dynamics were not run on several ranks.
- Tests: `tadah.lammps_mpi_dimer`, the `dimer` case of `check_pair_tadah_mpi.py` (`tests/mpi/run_dimer.in`): the published Tadah!N2 `DIMER` potential (`tests_data/pots/tadah.n2`, `compute_dimers`, newton off, `atom_style bond`) on 512 N2 molecules that the script writes (8x8x8 cells of 4 Å, centres moved by up to 0.3 Å, bond directions from a fixed seed, no molecule across the box edge), step 0 only, under the MPI lmp on 1, 2 and 4 ranks and on 4 ranks in a `4 1 1` grid (8 Å sub-domains, less than the ghost cutoff of 14 Å), against the reference run: forces, per-atom energies and virials, the energy and the pressure tensor to the step-0 tolerances of the other cases, and in every run, the reference included, the per-atom energies sum to the energy (to 1e-10 of it). The largest differences: forces 5e-14 eV/Å, pe/atom 1.1e-16 eV, the sum 2.2e-13 eV of -16.64 eV. A `compute_dimers` that sets each atom's per-atom energy instead of adding it fails the sum check in every run. Skipped like the other cases without `TADAH_LMP_MPI` and `TADAH_LMP_MPIEXEC`, and when the reference or the MPI lmp has no `atom_style bond` (the LAMMPS MOLECULE package; `lmp -h` lists the atom styles).
- `PairTadah::stage_extern(std::shared_ptr<tadah::LammpsTadah>)` / `clear_staged_extern()`: per-thread staging slot for the `EXTERN` potential handoff. Drivers that execute whole scripts through LAMMPS's `input->file()` (where per-line `load()` interleaving is impossible) stage the candidate potential once before the run; every `pair_coeff * * EXTERN ...` the script executes adopts it in `coeff()`. The slot lives outside the Pair object, so it survives `clear` cycles; precedence in `coeff()` is the staged slot, then a potential the driver gave the pair style with a `load()` overload, then an error (see the `pair_coeff * * EXTERN` entry under *Fixed*).
- Regression coverage for the `DM_Embed` scalar EAM interface and collapse tables. Existing capability-based generic and accelerated kernels accept the registered descriptor without name-specific branches.

### Changed
- **The sources are published at <https://github.com/tadah-project>.** CMake fetches cmake-tools and MD from `${TADAH_GIT_BASE}/<repository>.git`, and `configure` clones CORE, MODELS and MD from the same base: `https://github.com/tadah-project` unless `-DTADAH_GIT_BASE=<address>` (CMake) or the environment variable `TADAH_GIT_BASE` names another base, such as a mirror. Bugs and feature requests go to the issue tracker of this repository, `tadah.lammps`, and security issues to its private vulnerability reporting (`SECURITY.md`). The README carries no CI badges. Test: `tadah.lammps_git_base` (`ctest -L cmake`, cmake-tools' `tadah_add_git_base_check`).
- **The refusal of an in-source build states the rule it checks and gives an example**: `In-source builds are not allowed. The build directory must differ from the source directory (for example: cmake -S . -B build).` (1.5.0: `... Create a separate build directory and run CMake from there.`).
- **BREAKING (for scripts that read log.lammps) — a potential with keys that Tadah! 1.6.0 retired loads with one warning per `pair_coeff`.** `EWEIGHT`, `FWEIGHT`, `SWEIGHT`, `WDBFILE_AUTO` and `EWEIGHT_TEMP` weighted the training data of a fit before 1.6.0, `UNCERTAINTY` asked `tadah train` for `weights.tadah` and `ERROR` asked `tadah predict` for errors, uncertainty output, which is removed (Tadah.CORE): a potential that `tadah train` or `tadah hpo` of 1.0.0 to 1.5.0 wrote has `EWEIGHT`, `FWEIGHT` and `SWEIGHT` lines, from 1.3.0 a `WDBFILE_AUTO` line, one of `tadah train` an `UNCERTAINTY` line, and one of `tadah train` 1.5.0 an `ERROR` line when its configuration had one (the published Tadah!Kr1, Tadah!Kr2 and Tadah!N2 have the first three in their training record, from which no key is read). `WCOV` and `WNOISE`, which no release wrote, are retired keys too, and a potential with them loads with the same warning. `pair_coeff` loads such a potential as before, its energies and forces unchanged, stores none of the keys and writes one LAMMPS warning on rank 0 that lists them with their lines (`WARNING: pair tadah: potential 'pot.tadah': Retired keys EWEIGHT (line 8 of pot.tadah), FWEIGHT (line 10 of pot.tadah), SWEIGHT (line 23 of pot.tadah), UNCERTAINTY (line 25 of pot.tadah) and WDBFILE_AUTO (line 27 of pot.tadah) are ignored: ...`), each time it loads it. A potential trained by Tadah! 1.6.0 has none of them, and has `ESIGMA`, `FSIGMA`, `SSIGMA`, `ENORM` and `ENERGY` lines instead, which the `pair_style tadah` of Tadah! 1.5.0 and earlier refuses ("Invalid key"): potentials trained by this release need this release of the plugin. Tests: `test_retired_keys_load.cpp` (a potential written by Tadah! 1.5.0, `tests_data/pots/ta_blip_1.5.0.tadah`; the published Tadah!N2, `tests_data/pots/tadah.n2`, loads without the warning), `test_uncertainty_keys_load.cpp` (a potential with `WCOV` and `WNOISE`), Tadah.MLIP's `test_pair_coeff_reload.cpp` (one warning per `pair_coeff`, the energy of the file without the lines). The test potentials of `tests_data/pots` have no line of the retired keys, but for `ta_blip_1.5.0.tadah`.
- **BREAKING — `pair_style tadah` reads no run setting of a potential: an older potential's `VERBOSE` line no longer sets the Tadah log level** (Tadah.CORE's `run_setting_keys()`). `pair_coeff` loads the potential with `core::CoreUserConfigParser`, which stores no line of `VALIDATION`, `HPOTARGET`, `VERBOSE`, `NUMERIC`, `OUTFILE`, `AUDIT`, `AUDIT_SAMPLE` or `HEALTH_LOG` of a file whose key lines have `WEIGHTS`: a potential of an earlier version that has them (every 1.5.x `best_pot.tadah` has `HPOTARGET`, `VERBOSE` and `NUMERIC`, and `VALIDATION` when its run had a validation file) loads to the context of the potential without them, its models take their default verbosity, and the Tadah logger stays at the level LAMMPS leaves it at (`ERROR`, the logger's own). Storing the potential's `VERBOSE` set the logger's level for the rest of the LAMMPS process, on every rank: the `VERBOSE 1` of such a potential let Tadah's warnings through to standard output, `VERBOSE 2` its INFO lines too, and its models were built at its verbosity. The lines are ignored without a message: LAMMPS's screen and log.lammps are the input script's output, and no line of them changed a force or an energy. No code of Tadah.LAMMPS changed. Test: `pair_style tadah reads no run setting of an older potential, and its VERBOSE sets no log level` (`test_training_record_load.cpp`).
- **`pair_style tadah` writes the notices of a potential's keys that have no effect as LAMMPS warnings on rank 0**: a deprecated key (`SIGMA`, which Tadah! 1.0.0 to 1.5.0 wrote for an `OALGO 3` fit, `MBLOCK`, ...), `EMBF0` or `RHOFORM` on a potential without a DM_REAM block, a `WATOM` with a value other than 1 that no descriptor of the potential reads (D2_EAM, DM_EAM and the dummy descriptors read none: `Key WATOM (2.5) is ignored: no descriptor of the potential reads it ...`, Tadah.MODELS). `pair_coeff` loads the potential under a `core::IgnoredKeyNoticeCapture` and writes each notice through `error->warning`, naming the potential (`WARNING: pair tadah: potential 'pot.tadah': Deprecated key SIGMA is ignored: ...`), so it is in log.lammps and on the screen each time `pair_coeff` loads such a potential: every LAMMPS instance has it in its own log.lammps, also a second instance of the same process (the LAMMPS Python module in a loop, `tadah hpo`'s `PC_LAMMPS` evaluations; Tadah.CORE's capture gets each key once for itself). In 1.5.0 `SIGMA`, `MBLOCK`, `NBLOCK` and `MPIWPCKG` were ordinary keys and a `WATOM` that no descriptor reads had no effect, each without a warning, an `EMBF0` without a DM_REAM block gave no warning in LAMMPS, and a potential with `RHOFORM` did not load (an invalid key). Also for a driver's `load(Context)` at `pair_coeff * * EXTERN`, and before the error of a failed load. Tests: `test_uncertainty_keys_load.cpp`, HPO's `test_pair_tadah_notices.cpp`.
- **BREAKING — `pair_coeff` refuses a potential whose weights do not fit pair_style tadah's descriptor**, in one check (MODELS `require_weights_fit`, which `LammpsTadah::initialize` calls on every `pair_coeff`, a file or `EXTERN`, reported through `error->all`): the WEIGHTS count (`BF_Polynomial2`: D(D+1)/2 for D descriptor components; a kernel: one per basis vector), a kernel's basis vectors and, for a non-linear `NORM true` model, NMEAN and NSTDEV must fit the descriptor of DC_Selector (with BIAS true, one one-hot intercept column per ATOM species, then the two-body and many-body descriptors); a linear model's NMEAN and NSTDEV are not read and not checked. The predictors' dot products ran over the weights. A potential trained with BIAS true and two or more species by tadah before 1.6.0 has one intercept column shared by the species (1 + D WEIGHTS for N + D descriptor entries); pair_style tadah has built one per species since 1.3.0-beta.1, so with 1.3.0-beta.1 and later such a potential gave silently wrong energies and forces (a Ta-Nb D2_LJ potential: -622.5 eV/atom against -7.65 eV/atom from `tadah predict`), while with 1.2.0 and earlier, whose pair style used one shared column too, it matched `tadah predict`. It is refused, the message naming WEIGHTS, DSIZE, BIAS and ATOM and saying to retrain it (see Tadah.MLIP), and so is a linear potential with NORM true and BIAS false, whose weights an earlier version's conversion corrupted. `LammpsTadah` takes the Z -> intercept column table from CORE's `intercept_columns_by_Z`. Tests: `test_weights_layout.cpp`, `test_krr_basis_load.cpp`.
- `TADAH_LAMMPS_PAIR_STYLES` is **deprecated and ignored** — styles are discovered from the `PairStyle(...)` markers in the ML-TADAH headers. Setting it to a non-default value warns.
- Framework version `1.6.0`, read from CORE's `blueprint/blueprint_tadah.toml` after the Tadah dependencies are fetched, so the module reports the right version in every layout (sibling checkout or FetchContent). A missing blueprint is now a configure error instead of a silent `0.0.0`.
- Tests take Catch2 and its test discovery from cmake-tools (`tadah_setup_tests()`, `include(Catch)`); the vendored `tests/catch2/catch.hpp`, `tests/Catch.cmake` and `tests/CatchAddTests.cmake` are removed. Every discovered CTest entry now selects exactly its own test case and passes `--warn NoTests`, so an entry that matches no test case fails instead of passing. New test sources are picked up without a manual re-configure (`CONFIGURE_DEPENDS`).
- cmake-tools is fetched at `v1-dev` instead of `main`.
- **The `EAMFAST` load-time log line states where the collapse tables are accurate.** For the docs quickstart potential of 1.5.0 (a `D2_LJ` potential; the *Quick Start* potential of this release, in Tadah.MLIP, has a `DM_mBlipEnv` block and runs the generic kernel) it reads `Tadah! pair-collapse fast path ON (2b-only, nr=8192): table certified for r in [2.80125, 6.5] (rel tol 1e-9: energy 3e-13, force 1e-9); analytic below r=2.80125`; an EAM-form model reports a `phi` and a `rho` table. It printed `parity dphi=3.071e+42`: the largest absolute value error at about 64 segment midpoints, with no range, no force check and no threshold, dominated by the extrapolated head of the table. In `eam_collapse.h`, `TableCertificate` (`phi_cert`, `rho_cert`) replaces `parity_err_phi` / `parity_err_rho`, and `certify_table()`, `check_segment()` / `SegmentCheck`, `sample_table()`, `phi_analytic()`, `collapse_phi()` / `collapse_rho()`, `format_collapse_report()`, `kCollapseNR` (8192) and `kCollapseCheckIntervals` (32) are new. A table that misses the tolerance already in its top segment, just below the cutoff, has no certified range; the log then says so (`collapse fast path unavailable: phi table misses rel tol 1e-9 already in its top segment, just below the cutoff r=…; using the generic kernel`, or `rho table …`) and the generic kernel is used.
- **BREAKING — `pair_style tadah` refuses a `DIMER` potential whose descriptors name specific element pairs.** `compute_dimers` evaluates every atom as Z=1, whatever its element (with the pair weight of Z=1, not the `WATOM` weight of the element), so a `TYPE2B`/`TYPEMB` declared for `N N` applied to no pair and the model predicted from zero descriptors without a word. `LammpsTadah` now calls `models::require_dimer_element_pairs()` (MODELS 1.6.0) when it is built, so `pair_coeff` fails (`PairTadah::load` prints `DIMER evaluates every atom as Z=1; declare TYPE2B/TYPEMB with * * element pairs` and the line); a `DIMER` potential loads when its descriptors are declared for `* *`. Test: `test_dimer_element_pairs.cpp` (the published Tadah!N2 loads; `N N` and `* N` variants are refused).
- The README links and the pointers to the umbrella's community files (`CITATION.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, …) point to the `v1-dev` line: the `v1-dev` branch, `https://tadah.readthedocs.io/v1-dev/`. They pointed to `develop`.
- `pair_coeff` prints the DM_REAM embedding reference (`EMBF0 ZERO` = F(0) = 0 by construction, the new default; `EMBF0 FREE` = raw 1.5.0 embedding). The EAMFAST kernel needs no change: F comes from `DM_Base::eam_embed`, which applies the reference. Rebuild required (DM_Base/DM_REAM layout). New DM_REAM collapse tests (Blip5 and negative-knot Knot5 fixtures): `eam_embed` equals w·calc_aed, F(0) == 0.0 under ZERO.
- **BREAKING (via Tadah.MODELS) — a DM_REAM potential must declare its density form, `RHOFORM SIGNED | NONNEG | MONOTONE`.** `pair_coeff` refuses a DM_REAM potential without the key, and the LAMMPS error now carries the reason: `pair_coeff: failed to load Tadah potential from '<file>': DM_REAM: RHOFORM is required: ...` (screen, `log.lammps`, and the exception a library driver catches). Previously the error named only the file and the reason of any load failure went to stderr alone. A `pair_coeff * * EXTERN` line with nothing to adopt (no staged potential, and the driver's own `load()` of this pair style failed) appends the same reason: `pair_coeff * * EXTERN: no staged or pre-loaded Tadah potential (last load() failed: <reason>)`. `PairTadah` keeps the reason of the last failed load (`last_load_error()`, set by all four `load` overloads); the command-line overload that `pair_coeff` uses no longer prints it to stderr. **Migration:** a DM_REAM potential written before Tadah! 1.6.0 gives its 1.5.0 energies once the lines `RHOFORM SIGNED` and `EMBF0 FREE` are added.
- `pair_coeff` prints the density form next to the EMBF0 banner: `Tadah! DM_REAM density form RHOFORM <form>` with `(signed amplitudes, the density before tadah 1.6.0)`, `(rho >= 0 by construction)` or `(rho >= 0 and non-increasing by construction)`. The EAMFAST kernel needs no change: its rho table is sampled from, and certified against, `DM_Base::eam_density`, which evaluates the declared form, and below the certified range the kernel calls `eam_density` itself. **Rebuild required**: the DM_REAM layout changed (Tadah.MODELS), and `PairTadah` gains a member (declared last, so the offset of `lt` is unchanged). Tests: `test_eam_collapse.cpp` (the `MONOTONE` collapse: the rho table follows `eam_density` to a relative tolerance and is certified over its whole range; a potential without `RHOFORM` fails to load with the migration text, and an invalid value is an error at its line of the file); every DM_REAM test potential declares `RHOFORM SIGNED`.

### Fixed
- **A `pair_coeff * * <file>` line always loads its file**, replacing the potential the pair style held: an earlier file's, one a driver gave it with `load()`, or one an `EXTERN` line adopted (the staged slot keeps that one alive for the driver). `PairTadah::coeff` read a file only when the pair style held no potential, and LAMMPS keeps the pair style when `pair_style tadah` is given again, so a second `pair_coeff` of a script, with or without `pair_style tadah` before it, went on with the first potential without a message: a 250-atom Ta cell gave -2011.5331 eV with a D2_Knot5 + DM_REAM potential and then with the Quick Start potential, whose energy is -2011.4549 eV, and a two-species file after a one-species one was refused (`Symbol not in potential file: Nb`, looked up in the first potential). Since 1.0.0. Tests: Tadah.MLIP `test_pair_coeff_reload.cpp` (in-process LAMMPS).
- **`pair_coeff * * EXTERN` adopts a driver's potential only**: the one staged for the thread (`stage_extern()`), else one the driver gave the pair style with a `load()` overload, which every later `EXTERN` line adopts until a `pair_coeff` loads a file or adopts a staged potential. It is refused when the pair style holds the potential of an earlier `pair_coeff * * <file>` (`pair_coeff * * EXTERN: no staged or pre-loaded Tadah potential (pair_style tadah holds the potential of '<file>', loaded by an earlier pair_coeff; EXTERN adopts a potential the driver staged or gave this pair style with load())`), or the one an earlier `EXTERN` adopted from the staged slot, which the driver has cleared since (`... (pair_style tadah holds the potential an earlier pair_coeff * * EXTERN adopted from the staged slot, which the driver has cleared since; ...)`): both were adopted again without a word, so an `EXTERN` line in a plain `lmp` run after a file line ran the file's potential, and a LAMMPS instance kept without `clear` whose driver did not stage again ran the previous candidate. `tadah hpo` stages its candidate for every script with an `EXTERN` line and is not affected.
- **A `pair_coeff` that `pair_style tadah` refuses leaves it without a potential, and `run` refuses it**: `pair_style tadah has no potential: the last pair_coeff, or a driver's PairTadah::load(), failed (see its error)`. `PairTadah::coeff` releases the potential whenever it throws (a function-try-block). That covers a file that does not load, an element the potential does not have (`initialize()`) and an `EXTERN` line refused as above, and also the refusals raised before the file is read, which kept the potential the pair style held: a number of elements other than the number of LAMMPS types (`pair_coeff * * other.tadah Ta Ta` for one type, or no element), no file (`pair_coeff * *`) and every type `NULL` (`run` then stops at LAMMPS's `All pair coeffs are not set`). A potential a driver gave with `load()` is released by a malformed `EXTERN` line as well. A line LAMMPS refuses before it calls the pair style (`pair_coeff 1 1 ...`: `Pair_coeff must start with * * for pair style tadah`) does not reach it and leaves the potential as it was. In `lmp` the failed line stops LAMMPS; a program that drives LAMMPS as a library (the LAMMPS Python module) and goes on after the error ran with the potential the pair style held (the first one, after a failed second `pair_coeff`; one not mapped to the types, after an element it does not have), or died with SIGSEGV in `init_style()` (after a first `pair_coeff` whose file did not load).
- **The kernels size the per-atom descriptor and density arrays of the potential they evaluate by the arrays' own size** (`compute_2b_mb_half`, `compute_2b_mb_full`): the arrays belong to the potential object, and the pair style grew them only when `atom->nmax` exceeded a count of its own (`PairTadah::nmax`, removed), so the arrays of another object given to the same pair style stayed empty and the kernel wrote through them, SIGSEGV: an `EXTERN` line that adopts another staged object, or a driver's `load()`, in a pair style that has run (two staged objects of the Quick Start potential, or of an M_BLR BF_Polynomial2 potential in the full-list kernel, in a LAMMPS instance without `clear`), and with the fix above a second file. Tests: Tadah.MLIP `test_pair_coeff_reload.cpp`, both kernels.
- **The collapse tables of the fast path are reused for the element they were built for only** (`setup_eam_fastpath`, `EamCollapseTables::Z`): `pair_coeff * * EXTERN Nb` on the object that `EXTERN Ta` had tabled (one LAMMPS type, a potential of two elements) evaluated Nb with the tables of Ta (-223.94 eV against 0 for a potential with a Ta-Ta pair term only).
- **`LammpsTadah::initialize` maps a `NULL` type to no element** (Z 0, no intercept column, an intercept energy of 0) also on an object it initialised before; the type kept the element of the previous mapping, with its intercept column and energy. Test: `test_lammps_tadah_reinit.cpp`.
- **Rebuild required** for the fixes above: `PairTadah` has two members more (`lt_source_`, `lt_file_`, declared last) and none of `nmax`; the offset of `lt` is unchanged.
- **`compute_dimers` adds the energy of each molecule pair to the per-atom energy of its atoms** (`eatom`, `compute pe/atom`), as it adds it to the energy. With a `DIMER` potential an atom belongs to the cluster of its molecule with every molecule within the centre-of-mass cutoff, and its per-atom energy was set to its energy in each such cluster in turn, so it kept only that of the last one evaluated and the per-atom energies did not sum to the energy, on one rank or many (27 N2 molecules with Tadah!N2: sum(pe/atom) -0.036475 eV against pe -0.61539 eV). The energy, the forces and the virials were not affected. Tests: Tadah.MLIP `test_dimer_pe_atom.cpp` (the sum, and each atom's pe/atom in three molecules against its pe/atom in the two molecule pairs it belongs to).
- **A non-linear potential that does not fit its descriptor is refused when it is loaded** (`pair_coeff`, the check of the BREAKING entry under *Changed*), naming the keys: KRR basis vectors whose length is not the descriptor's (`BASIS holds SBASIS 2 basis vectors of 4 values each, but the descriptor of the potential has 3 components (DSIZE)`; every K(b, x) read past the shorter vector); through MODELS' `M_KRR_Core`, `WEIGHTS` without `SBASIS`/`BASIS` (the model predicted E = 0 for every atom) or with a number of weights other than the basis vectors, and a `BASIS` that `SBASIS` does not divide; and a `BF_Polynomial2` potential without D(D+1)/2 `WEIGHTS` for its D descriptor components (with fewer, every energy read past them; with more, the extra ones were ignored). Potentials of non-linear models trained with tadah 1.6.0 (MLIP) load, and `pair_style tadah` evaluates them as `tadah predict` does (HPO's `test_pair_tadah_nonlinear.cpp`: `BIAS false`, and `BIAS true` on one species; `test_multispecies_bias.cpp`: a `BF_Polynomial2` potential with `BIAS true` on two species), with `BIAS true` on several species too: tadah trains one intercept component per species, as the descriptor of `pair_style tadah` has. A potential trained before 1.6.0 with `BIAS true` on several species, with one bias component shared by the species, is refused with the instruction to retrain it. Test: `test_krr_basis_load.cpp`.
- **`pair_style tadah` with `BIAS true` sets the bias component for the atoms LAMMPS has, local and ghost** (`compute_2b_mb_full`, which every non-linear model takes, and `compute_2b_mb_half`, which a linear many-body model takes). The loops ran over every column of the descriptor array, `atom->nmax` of them, and read `atom->type` past the atoms, where LAMMPS does not set it: in a fresh process each such column got a 1, written through an index read from before the per-type table, and `run 0` in a second LAMMPS instance of the same process (as `tadah hpo` creates for `PC_LAMMPS` evaluations) died with SIGSEGV. LAMMPS must be rebuilt. Tests: HPO's `test_pair_tadah_nonlinear.cpp` (`pair_style tadah with BIAS true evaluates a trained model as the model predicts in every LAMMPS instance of a process`) and `test_multispecies_bias.cpp` (two species; `pair_style tadah sets the intercept components only for the atoms LAMMPS has, whatever atom->type holds past them`, which sets every type past the atoms to a valid one, so the old bound fails it whatever the memory held).
- **`compute_dimers` gives the descriptor of each atom of a molecule pair the intercept of that atom's species.** Column n holds the descriptor of the pair's atom IJ[n] (i1 i2 j1 j2), but its intercept was taken from the type of local atom n: with two species the intercepts were exchanged whenever the pair's atoms were not the first local atoms in order (two Nb-Ta molecules: -13.574 eV against the -13.552 eV of `tadah predict`). Tests: HPO `test_multispecies_bias.cpp`.
- **The `EAMFAST` fast kernels agree with the generic kernel to 1e-9 inside the certified range and exactly below it.** At load, each table is compared with the analytic function it samples, segment by segment from the cutoff down, and certified down to the smallest node `r_lo` above which every segment meets a relative tolerance of 1e-9 for the value and the derivative (energy and force). The errors are relative to the floor max(max|f| on [rc/2, rc], max|f| above the segment), so zero crossings and tapered cutoffs are handled and the scheme is unit-free. Each segment is sampled at 31 interior points, and its error between them is bounded by extrapolating from the neighbouring samples with a curvature allowance, because next to a C² knot (Blip bases, `KDEG` 3 knots) or the inner radius of `Cut_PolyS` / `Cut_CosS` the error peaks in a narrow band that a few check points per segment miss (three points understate it up to 3.5 times). The bound assumes the function is smooth on the scale of dr/32 apart from isolated knots and junctions, and it can exceed the true error, by up to 2 times next to a knot. `compute_2b_fast` and `compute_2b_mb_half_eamfast` evaluate the pair term analytically below `r_lo` (`phi_analytic()`, which also builds the table nodes, and the descriptor's `eam_density()` for `rho`). They used the table at every distance: for that `D2_LJ` quickstart potential its value was off by a factor of 130 at 1.2·10⁻³ Å and by 1.6·10⁻⁶ in the force at 0.1 Å. The certified range of that potential starts at 2.80 Å, below its bcc nearest-neighbour distance of 3.03 Å; closer in, the r⁻¹² term changes too quickly for a table of 8192 points to give the force to 1e-9 of the force scale near the minimum of the pair energy (2.68 Å). The certified range is one interval ending at the cutoff, so a single failing segment (a Blip knot, a switching-cutoff junction) ends it, and closer pairs cost as much as in the generic kernel. In 250-atom MD runs the fast path is 1.6 times (quickstart D2_LJ), 3.4 times (D2_LJ + DM_REAM) and 4.5 times (21-knot D2_Knot5) faster than the generic kernel.
- **`pair_style tadah` stops on two atoms at the same position**, naming their atom IDs (`error->one`: `pair_tadah: atom IDs 1 and 3 are at the same position ...`), in every kernel (`compute_dimers` of a `DIMER` potential included) and in `single()`; every force was nan (`run 0` completed with NaN forces, and dynamics carried on). LAMMPS must be rebuilt.
- **Pairs in the top table segment, just below a `Cut_Dummy` cutoff, got forces of order |phi(rc⁻)|/dr** (fmax 20.8 instead of 1.4 eV/Å on a rattled bcc cell with the `D2_LJ` quickstart potential of 1.5.0, and the same in `compute_2b_mb_half_eamfast`). `Cut_Dummy` is 0 at rc, and the top table node was sampled there, which pulled the whole segment [rc − dr, rc) towards 0. The top node now holds the limit from below; this also covers cutoffs for which (nr − 1)·dr rounds above rc. `compute_2b_fast` skips a pair at exactly rc, where the cutoff is 0, as the EAM kernel does.
- **ML-TADAH builds against LAMMPS with the runtime style registry (LAMMPS 2 Jun 2026 and later).** That LAMMPS generates `styles/style_pair.cpp` in place of `styles/style_pair.h`, so every configure with ML-TADAH failed with `ML-TADAH: Cannot find generated file: <build>/styles/style_pair.h`, and a LAMMPS configured without the integration hook built but stopped at run time with `ERROR: Unrecognized pair style 'tadah'`. The hook registers the ML-TADAH directory through LAMMPS's own style registration instead of editing a generated file. Both LAMMPS generations read that registration, and LAMMPS finds the styles from the `PairStyle(...)` markers in the ML-TADAH headers. LAMMPS 11 Feb 2026 and 2 Sep 2026 each configure and build with ML-TADAH and list `tadah` in `lmp -h`.
- **A configure-time assertion that the pair style was actually registered.** After LAMMPS finishes, the hook checks that a generated `style_pair.cpp`/`style_pair.h` really names `pair_tadah.h`, and hard-errors otherwise. A future LAMMPS reshuffle now stops the build instead of silently producing a binary that rejects `pair_style tadah` at run time.
- `pair_coeff * * EXTERN` with no staged or pre-loaded potential, and `pair_coeff` with an unloadable potential file, now raise a clean LAMMPS error. Previously `coeff()` discarded `load()`'s failure return and dereferenced the null potential — a segfault (this is how the legacy `input->file()`-based MPI worker died on EXTERN scripts).
- `README.md` and `integration/README.md` said `TADAH_GIT_BRANCH` selects the Tadah.LAMMPS branch and defaults to `main`. It is the git ref the Tadah modules are fetched at (Tadah.MD and, through it, MODELS and CORE); the Tadah.LAMMPS sources are the checkout at `TADAH_LAMMPS_SOURCE_DIR`. Without it the ref is detected from the checkout of the project CMake was started on, which in the LAMMPS build is LAMMPS (`stable`, `develop`, ...), so the READMEs now pass it (`-DTADAH_GIT_BRANCH=v1-dev`, or a release tag such as `v1.6.0`), in every configure example, and describe the detection. They clone Tadah.LAMMPS at `v1-dev` or a release tag by name (`git clone -b`): a plain clone checks out the repository's default branch. An example used a personal path.
- **`pair_coeff` warns when a DM_REAM potential has no `EMBF0` line.** Such a potential is evaluated with the default `EMBF0 ZERO` (F(0) = 0 by construction), which moves every atomic energy of a potential written by tadah 1.5.0 or earlier by -F(0) (for a non-linear model, forces change too). Every `pair_coeff` that loads such a potential prints a LAMMPS `WARNING`, on screen and in log.lammps, with the same text as the tadah notice, and the banner reads `EMBF0 ZERO (key absent: default ZERO, ...; add EMBF0 FREE to reproduce tadah <= 1.5.0 energies)`. Evaluation does not change. `LammpsTadah::embf0_declared` records whether the potential context as given stored the key, before DM_REAM materialises the default. **Rebuild required**: the `LammpsTadah` layout changed, and Tadah.HPO includes it.
- `pair_coeff` reports a failure of `LammpsTadah::initialize()` through `error->all` with its reason: `pair_coeff * * pot.tadah W` for an element the potential does not have stops with `ERROR: pair_coeff: failed to initialise Tadah potential 'pot.tadah': Symbol not in potential file: W` on the screen and in `log.lammps`, and in the exception a library driver catches. The exception escaped `coeff()`, so LAMMPS' `main` printed it to stderr and `log.lammps` recorded nothing. The `load(Context)` overloads no longer print a failure to stderr: `pair_coeff * * EXTERN` reports `last_load_error()` through `error->all`. Tests: `test_eam_collapse.cpp` (the reason `initialize()` gives), and through LAMMPS HPO's `test_ream_fastpath.cpp` (this module's tests have no LAMMPS instance).

## [1.4.0] — 2026-07-14

### Added
- **EAM-collapse fast path** (`compute_2b_mb_half_eamfast`, new header `eam_collapse.h`): for a linear model whose descriptors are a radial two-body block plus an EAM-form many-body block, single species (the `tadah refit` family, e.g. `D2_Knot5` + `DM_REAM`), the potential collapses exactly to scalar pair/density/embedding functions. The pair style evaluates cubic-Hermite `phi`/`rho` tables sampled from the descriptors' own calc routines plus the analytic embedding, with scalar (1-double) ghost communication instead of descriptor-vector comm. ~7x faster force calls on the refit family; agrees with the generic kernel to ~1e-9 relative (a mid-node parity self-check is logged at load). Controlled by the `EAMFAST` config key, **default on**; set `EAMFAST false` in the potential/config for the bitwise generic path. Non-collapsible models (nonlinear, normalised, bias, multi-species, dimer, non-EAM-form MB) silently keep the generic kernel. Adjoint capture (`capture_adjoint_state`) with the fast path active is a hard error — such drivers must set `EAMFAST false`.
- `PairTadah::load(std::shared_ptr<tadah::LammpsTadah>)`: drivers (e.g. `tadah hpo`) can build ONE potential object per candidate and share it across `clear`/`pair_coeff EXTERN` cycles instead of reconstructing it per `pair_coeff`. The object carries per-simulation scratch, so sharing is valid only across sims that run sequentially (per-thread).

### Changed
- `PairTadah::lt` is now a `std::shared_ptr` (was `unique_ptr`); all existing `load` overloads keep their semantics.

## [1.3.0-beta.1] — 2026-05-20

A small but focused release: a per-species bias precomputation, the `i`/`j` swap fix, and the standard build / community / VERSION wiring.

### Added

## [1.1.1] and earlier (condensed)

The `v1.0.0` → `v1.1.1` releases were the "internal playground" era. The LAMMPS pair-style integration matured during this period: CMake-driven LAMMPS build, integration tests, ghost-atom NN-list correctness fixes, and `D2_mJoin` / `compute_2b` wiring. `v1.1.1` was the last tag in this era and is the version on which downstream LAMMPS users have been running until this beta. No published changelog covers this era; treat anything before `1.3.0-beta.1` as undocumented in the modern sense.
