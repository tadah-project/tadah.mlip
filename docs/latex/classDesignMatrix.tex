\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
\subsubsection*{template$<$typename Mode$>$\newline
class Design\+Matrix$<$ Mode $>$}

Design Matrix $ \Phi $ and the corresponding target vector $ \mathrm{T} $

An aggregation matrix composed of \textbackslash{}phi\+\_\+i matrices \begin{DoxyVerb}Phi = | \phi_1 |
      | \phi_2 |
      | ...    |
      | \phi_N |
\end{DoxyVerb}


where N corresponds to the number of structures in a provided \mbox{\hyperlink{structStructureDB}{Structure\+DB}}.

The ordering in $ \phi_i $ \begin{DoxyVerb}| energy of the 1st structure
| force on the 1st atom in the x-dir
| force on the 1st atom in the y-dir
| force on the 1st atom in the z-dir
| force on the 2st atom in the x-dir
| ...
| force on the n-th atom in the z-dir
| stress xx
| stress xy
| stress xz
| stress yy
| stress yz
| stress zz
\end{DoxyVerb}


We need to know\+: \mbox{\hyperlink{classConfig_STRESS}{S\+T\+R\+E\+SS}}, \mbox{\hyperlink{classConfig_FORCE}{F\+O\+R\+CE}}

Scalling factors \mbox{\hyperlink{classConfig_FWEIGHT}{F\+W\+E\+I\+G\+HT}}, \mbox{\hyperlink{classConfig_SWEIGHT}{S\+W\+E\+I\+G\+HT}}

Default scalling\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Forces are scaled by 1/\+N\+\_\+i/3 where N\+\_\+i is a number of atoms in the i-\/th structure.
\item Stresses are scaled by 1/6.
\item Energies are scaled by 1/\+N\+\_\+i
\end{DoxyItemize}
