## [1.6.0] — unreleased

### Added
- **The rule of an OPTIM box on a key comes from the lower limit the descriptors declare for its values** where no density-form or bump rule applies (`rhoform.h`). The limits are one per key: every value > 0 for `SGRID2B`, `SGRIDMB`, `SEMBFUNC` and `RCUTENV` (the limit `kHardLoPositive`, 1e-6, `param_meta.h`), and every value >= 0 for `RCUT2B`, `RCUTMB`, `CGRID2B` and `CGRIDMB`. `declared_low_bound_rule(key)` is the rule of the limit that the registered descriptors declare for the values of `key` (`ParamMeta::hard.lo` of a parameter with `arg_pos <= 0`). `optim_box_low_bound_rule(key, ctx[, entry])` is `optim_low_bound_rule(key, ctx[, entry])` when that gives a rule, else the rule of the limit that the descriptor of `ctx` reading the key declares, named as its subject (`the DM_mBlipEnv descriptor requires every RCUTENV value > 0`). On a joined line, a key the line splits (its `RCUT` and `RCTYPE` values and the list keys of its descriptors) takes the rule of the descriptor whose entry it is. Any other key, which the line gives every descriptor whole, takes the rule of a descriptor that declares a limit for it: `RCUTENV` of `DM_mJoin DM_mBlip ... DM_mBlipEnv ...` has the rule of `DM_mBlipEnv` in either order. `OptimLowBoundRule` has the `bound` of a `>=` rule (a `>` rule has the bound 0), and its `requirement` is a `std::string`. Its `clamp_low(seed)` is the low end of an AUTO box that reaches below the rule: the bound of a `>=` rule, and half the seed for a `>` rule. That is `positive_box_low(seed, bound)`, half-way from the bound to a seed above it, the low end at which MLIP's refit writer clamps the box of a width too. `tadah hpo` and `tadah analysis check_bounds` hold every OPTIM box to `optim_box_low_bound_rule`. 1.5.0 had no such rule: it searched an explicit box below the limit, and an `AUTO` box of a width or a centre that reached below it (it took no `AUTO` box of `RCUTENV`); an evaluation below 0 of `RCUTENV` failed (`Cutoff distance cannot be negative.`). Test: `test_optim_low_bound.cpp`.
- **`D_Base::reads_watom()` and `D_Base::depends_on(theta_name, arg_pos)`**: whether the values of a descriptor (its descriptor entries and their position derivatives) are weighted by `WATOM`, and whether they depend on value `arg_pos` (1-based, the `OPTIM` index) of the search parameter `theta_name` (a key, or a component path), whatever the atoms of a structure. `WATOM` counts when the value weighs a pair of the descriptor's elements (`D_Base::watom_value_weighs_a_pair`: per species, a pair with that element; in the pair form, that pair); so do the keys of the descriptor, its cutoff key (`RCUT2B`, `RCUTMB`; a `DM_Embed` block depends on its own `CUTOFF` path instead), the `TYPE2B` parameters of `D2_ZBL` and `D2_MIE`, `RCUTENV` of `DM_mBlipEnv` (`F_Base::scalar_keys()`) and the component paths of a `DM_Embed` block's ID; `D2_mJoin` and `DM_mJoin` depend on what a descriptor they join depends on, and the dummies on nothing. `reads_watom()` is false for `D2_EAM`, `DM_EAM`, `D2_Dummy` and `DM_Dummy`, and true for every other descriptor, also one that does not override it. The analytical gradient of `tadah hpo` claims the rows of a parameter only where every descriptor that depends on it differentiates it (Tadah.MLIP, Tadah.HPO).
- **The reach of a cutoff and of a radial density channel** (`descriptors/density_reach.h`): `kDensityReachFloor` (1e-6), the one value below which a radial function at a neighbour distance, or the density of an atom, counts as zero; `neighbour_range(data, rcut)`, the neighbour distances a density with cutoff `rcut` sums over; `gaussian_reach_width(d)` and `blip_reach_width(d)`, the width above which a Gaussian or a blip is below that floor at distance `d` from its centre; `cutoff_reach_check(data, seed, key)`, the check of the cutoff of every descriptor (the density cutoff `RADIUS` of a `DM_Embed` component included), and `radial_reach_check(data, seed, kind)`, the `CGRIDMB` check of the densities (below); `cutoff_suggestion`, the suggested range of a cutoff (`RCUT2B`, `RCUTMB`), and `gaussian_centre_suggestion`, `gaussian_width_suggestion`, `blip_centre_suggestion` and `blip_width_suggestion`, the `CGRIDMB` and `SGRIDMB` suggestions of the densities, which read the seed (below); `no_pair_suggestion(seed)`, the row without an `OPTIM` line of data without a pair of atoms within reach, naming the element pairs of the descriptor's line; and `kCutoffSearchReach` (1.25), the factor by which `tadah analysis suggest_bounds` takes the neighbour statistics past the largest cutoff of the seed (Tadah.MLIP).
- **`SeedView(context, first_entries)` and `SeedView::entry_number(key, i)`** (`param_meta.h`): a view of the context of one descriptor of a joined `TYPE2B` or `TYPEMB` line (`D_mJoin::parse_context`), which holds its share of each list key, with the number of values of each key that come before its own; `entry_number` gives the 1-based number of a value in the whole key, as an `OPTIM` line names it, and `radial_reach_check` names a channel by it (`CGRIDMB(3) = 30`, the second channel of a `DM_Blip` joined after a `DM_EAD` of one channel). A view of a whole configuration numbers from 1. `SeedView(context, first_entries, pairs)` and `SeedView::line_pairs()` give a view the element pairs of the descriptor's line, which the texts of its checks and suggestions name (below).
- **`LinePairs`** (`descriptors/line_pairs.h`): the element pairs a descriptor's `TYPE2B` or `TYPEMB` line names, from its element tokens (`D_Base::get_init_atoms()`; `Ta Ta Ta W`: (Ta, Ta) and (Ta, W), each in either order; `*` names every element), `names(a, b)`, `every()` (`* *`), `text()` as a message names them (`(Ta, Ta) or (Ta, W)`) and `stats(data)`, the neighbour statistics of those pairs alone (Tadah.MLIP: the pre-flight audit and `tadah analysis check_bounds` give them to a descriptor's checks, `tadah analysis suggest_bounds` those of each pair to its suggesters). An odd number of tokens is refused (`the element types of a descriptor's line come in pairs, and the line has 3 of them, Ta Ta W`).
- **`ParamMeta::reach_each_pair`** (`param_meta.h`): marks a parameter each value of which must reach each element pair of the descriptor's line, as the check of a cutoff judges it (`cutoff_reach_check`); every `RCUT2B` and `RCUTMB` is marked. For a line of several pairs, `tadah analysis suggest_bounds` (Tadah.MLIP) takes the range of such a parameter from the largest low end of the ranges of the pairs to the largest high end, and that of any other parameter as the range that holds the range of each pair. Test: `The cutoff of every descriptor is marked to reach each element pair of its line`.
- **`D_mJoin::check_potential_lines(context, command)`**: the refusal of a key of a potential's descriptors set on two lines of one potential file, and of `TYPE2B` or `TYPEMB` lines other than those tadah writes, for a command that reads the potential (Tadah.MLIP: every command that reads a configuration file with `-c`, and `tadah refit --edit`), below.
- **`BoundSuggestion`** (`param_meta.h`): what a `BoundSuggester` returns, a range (`first`, `second`, as the pair it extends, which converts to it) with its rationale (the form it assumes and the data it comes from), `fixed` for a range that is not derived from the data (`fixed_range()`), and `none` for an entry without an `OPTIM` line (`no_row()`). A suggester returns no range of zero width (below).
- **`eam_lattice::reach_distance(lattice, a0, reach_smin)` and `require_crystal_on_grid(lattice, a0, reach_smin, dr, rcut)`** (`eam_lattice.h`): the nearest-neighbour distance of the crystal compressed to a/a0 = `reach_smin`, which `EAMRefit::analyze_reachability` takes as `r_reach`, and the crystal check of `tadah refit` (MLIP's `RefitEngine::run()`): `require_crystal`'s rules (a known lattice, a0 > 0); then a crystal whose nearest neighbours at a0 are at or beyond the table's cutoff `rcut` is refused (LAMMPS, `pair_style eam/alloy`, counts a pair only below `rcut`, so in LAMMPS the crystal has no interaction; tadah's `neighbour_distances`, D2_EAM and DM_EAM keep a pair at exactly `rcut`: `tadah refit: the lattice constant of the EAM header, a0 = 361 A, puts the nearest neighbours of the fcc crystal 255.26554800834364 A apart, at or beyond the table's cutoff, rcut = 5 A, and LAMMPS (pair_style eam/alloy) counts a pair only below rcut: the crystal has no interaction in LAMMPS. The a0 of the element line of the setfl file (...) is the equilibrium lattice constant in Angstrom (a value in picometres is 100 times the value in Angstrom).`), and so is one whose nearest neighbours at a/a0 = `REFIT_REACH_SMIN` are less than the step `dr` of the table's radial grid apart, naming a0, the lattice, the distance, `REFIT_REACH_SMIN` and `dr` (`tadah refit: the lattice constant of the EAM header, a0 = 1e-323 A, puts the nearest neighbours of the fcc crystal 0 A apart at the strongest compression the refit tests (a/a0 = REFIT_REACH_SMIN 0.1), less than the step of the table's radial grid, dr = 0.0125313 A: the table describes no pair distance of that crystal. The a0 of the element line of the setfl file (...) is the equilibrium lattice constant in Angstrom.`), so `dr` <= `r_reach` < `rcut` for every table it passes. Tests: `test_eam_lattice.cpp`.
- **`Function_Base::mparams()`** (`MParamSlot`: name, integer, positive): the `MPARAMS` values a basis function or kernel reads when it is built, in order. `Kern_RBF`: `sigma` (positive); `Kern_Polynomial`: `d` (an integer, positive), `gamma`, `c`; `Kern_Sigmoid`: `gamma`, `c`; every other function none (the default). Tadah.HPO checks `OPTIM MPARAMS` against it at startup. Test: `test_function_mparams.cpp`.
- **`shared_setfl(path)`** (`eam_setfl_io.h`): the setfl file read and splined once for every caller of the process that reads the same file (the path as given, in the working directory it names) while its bytes are unchanged; a call reads the file and parses it again when its contents changed (a table already handed out is not changed). `EAMSetflIO::read_setfl_text(text, path)` parses the contents of a setfl file. Test: `test_eam_setfl_cache.cpp`.
- **`log_warning_once(key, message)`** and `warning_logged_once(key)` (`descriptors/warn_once.h`): a Logger WARNING at most once per process for a key, used by the descriptor warnings below; a call while the Logger hides WARNINGs does not use up the key.
- **`D_Base::cutoff_fixed_by(i)`**: what fixes value i of a descriptor's cutoff key (`RCUT2B`, `RCUTMB`), as a clause for a message, or "" for a value the descriptor takes as a free parameter (the default). D2_EAM and DM_EAM answer the cutoff of their setfl file for every value (`setfl_cutoff_clause`: `D2_EAM takes the cutoff 5.58190742024 of its setfl file 'Ta.eam.alloy' (SETFL)`; "" when built with `INIT2B`/`INITMB` false); D2_mJoin and DM_mJoin answer with the joined descriptor that takes value i. Tadah.HPO refuses `OPTIM RCUT2B`/`RCUTMB` on such a value at startup. Test: `test_eam_descriptors.cpp`.

- **`KernelSettingsLineScope`** (`functions/kernels/kern_base.h`): while one is open, a kernel built at `VERBOSE` > 0 prints its settings line (`Kern_RBF | sigma: 1 | gamma: 1`; `Kern_Polynomial`, `Kern_Sigmoid`, `Kern_Quadratic`, `Kern_LQ`) at the first build of its kind only, with that build's settings (`Lines::FirstOfEachKernel`, keyed on the kernel's label, not on its values), or at no build (`Lines::None`). Tadah.HPO holds a scope for each `tadah hpo` run, which builds the model of every evaluation from one configuration and printed the line for each (9 lines for 4 evaluations with one `PC_LAMMPS` script; see Tadah.HPO). `Kern_Base::print_settings(label, settings)` prints the line for the kernels; its text is unchanged, its numbers formatted as `std::cout` formats them. - **`zero_rows(Phi, rows)` and `zero_rows(Phi, rows, factors)`** (`zero_rows.h`): which data rows of a design matrix are zero in every column, a value of at most `kZeroRowTolerance` (1e-10) times the largest magnitude of its column counting as zero, so that the rounding of sums that cancel (the force rows of an atom at a centre of symmetry, about 1e-16) does not decide whether a row counts. With the row factors of a fit the largest magnitudes are taken over the rows of factor above 0, and a row of factor 0 or less is zero: a row the fit leaves out (priority 0) does not decide whether another row is zero. LinearRegressor's count of non-zero data rows and Tadah.MLIP's count of the observations of a fit (with the factors) take it from there. Tests: `test_zero_rows.cpp`, `test_linear_regressor_messages.cpp` (`LinearRegressor: OALGO 2 and 4 count a data row of rounding residues as zero`).
- **`M_KRR_Train::train2(B, T, error_scale)`**: the solve of M_KRR implementation 2 takes the error scale σ of its targets (default 1, the unit of T) and solves (K + `LAMBDA` σ² I) w = T, the kernel ridge regression of the residuals over σ, so `LAMBDA` weighs against the residuals in units of σ as in every other fit (Tadah.MLIP passes `ESIGMA` in eV); an error scale that is not a finite number > 0 is a `std::logic_error`. Test: `test_krr_train2_linear.cpp`.
- **`LinearRegressor::train(..., intercepts, n_obs)`**, which takes the number of observations (the data rows of priority above 0 that are not zero in every column, without the ridge-augmentation rows); the overloads without `n_obs` take `rows - cols`, the observations of a design matrix with its augmentation rows and no rows of priority 0 (`augmented_observations`). `M_BLR_Train::train(Phi, T, n_obs)` and `M_KRR_Train::train(Phi, T, n_obs)` likewise. `OLS::pseudoinverse_threshold(S, k, rcond)` is the rank threshold of `solveWithPseudoinverse`, and `LinearRegressor::dropped_directions(svd, rcond)` the number of directions it leaves out of the weights: an `OALGO 3` fit names them in a WARNING (`OALGO 3: 1 of 4 singular directions of the design matrix are at or below the rank threshold (OALGO's rcond 1e-06 times the largest singular value): the solve leaves them out of the coefficients.`), a fit WARNING (`FitWarningKind::SingularDirectionsDropped`, `fit_warning()`) that `tadah train` prints and `tadah hpo` gives once per run in its journal, which for an rcond of 0 or less names the threshold 0, the threshold the solve then uses (`... at or below the rank threshold (0, as OALGO's rcond -1 is not above 0): ...`). Tests: `test_oalgo3_fit.cpp`, `test_oalgo3_solve.cpp`, `test_rank_threshold.cpp`.
- **`Function_Base::weights_count(dsize)` and `basis_vector_size()`**: the number of model weights (the design-matrix width) for a descriptor of `dsize` entries — `dsize` for `BF_Linear` and `Kern_Linear`, `dsize (dsize + 1) / 2` for `BF_Polynomial2`, the number of basis vectors for a kernel with a basis — and the length of a kernel's basis vectors (0 without a basis). MLIP's `get_phi_cols` delegates to `weights_count`, so the formula lives in one place. With BIAS true, `BF_Polynomial2` keeps its `dsize (dsize + 1) / 2` products; the N (N - 1) / 2 products of two different intercept columns are identically zero (an atom has one species), so their weights have no data (none for one species). Tests: `test_weights_fit.cpp`.
- **`require_weights_fit(ctx, dsize, f[, sizes])`, `trained_sizes(ctx)`, `is_linear_function(f)`** (`functions/weights_fit.h`; a legacy multi-species BIAS potential and a linear NORM true BIAS false one are refused): the one check of a potential's fit, shared by MLIP and `pair_style tadah`. It refuses a potential whose `WEIGHTS` count is not `f.weights_count(dsize)` (`the potential has 3 WEIGHTS, but BF_Linear needs 4 weights for a descriptor of 4 components (DSIZE)`), whose kernel basis vectors do not have `dsize` entries (`BASIS holds SBASIS 5 basis vectors of 3 values each, but the descriptor of the potential has 4 components (DSIZE)`), or, for a non-linear model with NORM true, whose `NMEAN`/`NSTDEV` do not have `dsize` values (a linear model, `BF_Linear` or `Kern_Linear`, predicts with converted weights and never reads them, so they are not checked); the message names WEIGHTS (NMEAN, NSTDEV, BASIS), DSIZE, BIAS and ATOM. When BIAS is true with N >= 2 species and the sizes are those of one intercept column instead of N (how tadah trained BIAS true before 1.6.0), the message says so and to retrain: `pair_style tadah` has used one intercept column per species since 1.3.0-beta.1, so such a potential gave wrong LAMMPS energies and forces with 1.3.0-beta.1 and later (with 1.2.0 and earlier the pair style used one shared column too). It also says to retrain a non-linear NORM potential with 2 x DSIZE `NMEAN`/`NSTDEV` values (what `tadah train -c <potential>` appended before 1.6.0; the model read the first DSIZE, the other potential's). A linear potential with NORM true and BIAS false is refused whatever its sizes: tadah no longer trains it, and the conversion that wrote one had no intercept column for the normalisation's constant. M_KRR `Kern_Linear` (until 1.6.0) and M_BLR `BF_Linear` (up to 1.2.0) took descriptor component 0 for an intercept, so such a potential's energies and forces are wrong; M_BLR `BF_Linear` from 1.3.0-beta.1, which `tadah hpo` and the API wrote (`tadah train` refused the configuration), dropped the mean correction, so its energies are off by the same constant for every atom and its forces are right; the message says which, for the potential's function. Such a potential needs a retrain. MLIP calls it where weights are used to predict and `pair_style tadah` at `pair_coeff`; never where a model is built for training. Tests: `test_weights_fit.cpp`.
- `require_dimer_element_pairs(context, d2, dm)` (`descriptors/dimer_element_pairs.h`) and `D_Base::applies_to_every_element_pair()`. The `DIMER` evaluators (MLIP's `DescriptorsCalc::calc_dimer`, `pair_style tadah`'s `compute_dimers`) evaluate every atom as Z=1, so a `TYPE2B`/`TYPEMB` descriptor declared for specific element pairs (`N N`) applied to no pair they see, and the model predicted from zero descriptors without a word (`tadah predict` gave 0 eV for the published Tadah!N2 potential with `N N` pairs). With `DIMER` on, each switched-on, non-empty descriptor must apply to every element pair, i.e. be declared with `* *`; otherwise the function throws `DIMER evaluates every atom as Z=1; declare TYPE2B/TYPEMB with * * element pairs` and names the line. A `D2_mJoin`/`DM_mJoin` applies to every pair when each descriptor it joins does. MLIP and Tadah.LAMMPS call it when they load a potential. Test: `test_dimer_element_pairs.cpp`.
- `embf0_absent_consequence(bool linear)` (`embf0.h`): the text that explains what a trained DM_REAM potential without an `EMBF0` line means under the default `ZERO` and how to migrate (`EMBF0 FREE`). DM_REAM's once-per-process notice builds its WARNING from it, and the LAMMPS pair style prints the same text in its `pair_coeff` WARNING.
- **`rhoform.h`**, the DM_REAM density-form helpers (modelled on `embf0.h`): `enum class RhoForm { Signed, NonNeg, Monotone }`, `DensityFormError` (a `std::runtime_error` thrown by every density-form and density validation, so code that probes a model and swallows construction errors can rethrow it), `rhoform_name`, `rhoform_is_positive`, `parse_rhoform` (case-insensitive), `rhoform_absent_message()` (the one migration text DM_REAM, HPO and the LAMMPS pair style print), `stored_rhoform` / `require_rhoform` (stored values only, never insert), `normalise_rhoform_key` (writers: a DM_REAM context without the key throws, the value is canonicalised to upper case, a stray key on a context without DM_REAM is removed and reported once per process on standard error), `check_rhoform_precedence` (engines merging a run's configuration over a potential, `tadah refit --edit` with `-c`: a different form, or a form for a DM_REAM potential without one, is refused naming the potential, `RHOFORM conflict: pot.tadah declares RHOFORM SIGNED and the configuration file sets RHOFORM MONOTONE. ...`), `rhoform_cutoff_whitelisted`, and `OptimLowerBound` / `optim_key_lower_bound` (`AMPGRIDMB` is non-negative under `NONNEG`/`MONOTONE`; shared by the MLIP hpotarget writer and the HPO AUTO boxes).
- **`ReamDensity`** (`ream_density.h`; every method out of line in `src/ream_density.cpp`, compiled with MODELS' own flags as DM_REAM's density loops were; in a MODELS compiled without FMA contraction, the presets' flags on x86-64 (no `-march=native`, no `-ffast-math`), which the goldens check, the SIGNED sums are, on the same system, the density before 1.6.0 bit for bit and the relations below are bitwise; a MODELS compiled with FMA contraction, `-march=native` on an FMA machine or AArch64, gives them to rounding only, and a `-march=native` unit that compiled the sums would fuse multiply-adds into FMAs): the one evaluator of the DM_REAM pair density in its form. `from_context` (stored values only), `validate`, `value`, `deriv`, `value_deriv` (bitwise the separate calls), `scan_grid` (numpy-exact `linspace` grid, bump-major sums that are bitwise `value()` point by point, extremes with ties to the first index, optional per-point term magnitudes) and `rhobar_scan` (lattice density over neighbour shells, rising steps counted). `DM_REAM::density()` exposes it and the non-virtual `DM_REAM::pair_density(Zi, Zj, r)` returns exactly `calc_rho`'s increment; the plugin ABI (`DM_Base`) is unchanged.
- Integrated-bump step functions (`d_basis_functions.h`): `SB`/`dSB`/`d2SB` (Blip, `Q(a) = (2-a)^4 - 4(1-a)_+^4`, S = Q/24 or 1 - Q/24), `SB5`/`dSB5`/`d2SB5`/`SB5_value_deriv` (Blip5, `P(a) = (3-a)^6 - 6(2-a)_+^6 + 15(1-a)_+^6`, S = P/720 or 1 - P/720) and `SG`/`dSG`/`d2SG` (Gaussian, `0.5 erfc(sqrt(eta)(r - mu))`), evaluated as truncated-power products (verified against 60-digit quadrature: tail within 1.4 ulp relative, plateau within 5.4e-17, S(mu) = 1/2 exactly); `radial_step_*` dispatchers in `basis_dispatch.h` (the knot family throws).
- `nnls()` (`nnls.h`): Lawson-Hanson non-negative least squares with row weights, a column mask and Tikhonov rows, the passive-set subproblems solved by Householder QR of the passive columns (never the normal equations: step columns reach cond ~4e9) with an incrementally updated factorisation; returns the passive set (for variable-projection Jacobians) and whether the dual test converged. Deterministic (no BLAS, no threads).
- `EAMRefit::fit_window_r_lo()` and `fit_window_rho_hi()`: the windows the fit used, phi and rho on [`fit_window_r_lo()`, rcut] and F on [0, `fit_window_rho_hi()`]. `fit()` records its radial window (`min(max(r_lo, r_K), 0.8 r_reach)` under placement `window`, `r_lo` under `uniform`, and never below `r_lo`, where its samples and its grid start) and its embedding window (`rho_reach`, or `rhomax` under `uniform`); `finalize_injected(rwin_lo, rho_win_hi)` records its arguments, the compact fitter's windows; both are 0 before either. The window was a local of `fit()`, and `tadah refit` reported 0.9 r_reach for it (Tadah.MLIP). The fits are unchanged. Tests: `test_eam_refit.cpp`.
