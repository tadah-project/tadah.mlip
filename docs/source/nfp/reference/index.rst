.. _nfp_reference:

Reference
=========

The pages below list every line, block and key of a target file, every
optimiser and every :hpo:key:`OPTIMIZER.PARAM` key, as ``tadah hpo`` reads
them. The entry of a key gives its syntax and values and, where they apply,
its default, where it may appear, its repeat rule, its rules, the messages
of the checks ``tadah hpo`` makes on it and examples.
The entry of an optimiser says what it is, what it reads and how it
searches. The :ref:`nested_fitting` page introduces nested fitting and its
input files.
